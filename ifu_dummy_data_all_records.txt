--- 3.2  Intended Use / Intended Purpose ---
Intended use: The device is intended for internal fixation of fractures and osteotomies of long bones, including the tibia, femur, humerus, radius and ulna, in conjunction with bone plates or as a standalone lag screw.
Intended user: Orthopaedic surgeons trained in internal fracture fixation, operating in a sterile surgical environment such as an operating theatre.
Intended patient population: Skeletally mature adult patients with fractures requiring internal fixation, with adequate bone quality and no active infection at the surgical site.

--- 3.3  Indications & Contraindications ---
Indications for use: - Fixation of diaphyseal and metaphyseal fractures of long bones
    - Arthrodesis
    - Osteotomy fixation
    - Fixation of bone fragments and grafts
Contraindications: - Active or suspected infection at the surgical site
    - Known hypersensitivity to titanium or titanium alloy
    - Insufficient bone quantity or quality to support the implant
    - Patients unwilling or unable to follow post-operative care instructions

--- 3.4  Warnings & Precautions ---
Warnings: Do not use if the package is damaged or the sterile barrier is breached. For single use only. Do not reuse. Do not resterilise. Reuse may cause infection, implant failure or patient injury.
Precautions: Select the correct screw length and diameter using the depth gauge. Avoid over-tightening, which can strip the bone thread. Use only compatible instruments. Do not bend or notch the screw.
Residual risks: Infection, loosening, screw breakage, delayed union or non-union, soft tissue irritation, and the need for revision surgery.

--- 3.5  Instructions for Use (Directions) ---
Preparation: Before use, inspect the package for damage and check the expiry date. Open the package using aseptic technique and verify the product code and lot number against the label.
Step-by-step use: 1. Expose the fracture site and reduce the fracture.
    2. Drill a 2.5 mm pilot hole through both cortices.
    3. Measure the required screw length with the depth gauge.
    4. Tap the hole with the 3.5 mm tap.
    5. Insert the screw with the hexagonal screwdriver until firmly seated.
    6. Confirm position and fixation under imaging.
After use: Record the lot number in the patient file and complete the implant card. Dispose of unused opened screws as bio-medical waste.
